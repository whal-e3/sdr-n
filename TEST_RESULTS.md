## Real-GP diagnostic and source limits

The desktop diagnostic sampled `2026-09-26T15:00:00Z`, `2026-09-27T03:00:00Z`, and `2026-09-27T14:59:59Z`, covering the start, noon, and final second of September 27 in Asia/Seoul. Of those, 28 propagated successfully at their original epochs but failed when extrapolated to the current day: 18 with evolved eccentricity outside the model domain and 10 with nonfinite positions/ranges. JWST (50463), NGRST (100532), and FALCON HEAVY R/B (100533) failed model construction even at their epochs. Direct raw-TLE propagation reproduced all 31 failures, supporting stale-element/model-domain limits rather than an OMM conversion fault.
